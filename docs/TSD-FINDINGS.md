# Troy School District and MPSERS: budgets, actuals, and what full funding would mean

Research date: 2026-11-18. Sources: the TSD Board of Education document archive (tsd-boarddocs.karpowitsch.org), through the FY2026 audit posted for the 2026-11-10 workshop. Every figure carries a source tag like **[S1 p53/36]**: source S1, PDF page 53, printed page 36. The source list is in section 9.

Conventions:
- **TSD FY** runs July 1 to June 30. "FY2026" means July 2025 to June 2026.
- **MPSERS year** runs October 1 to September 30. MPSERS rates change every October, so each TSD fiscal year carries 3 months of one MPSERS rate year and 9 months of the next.
- **"Calc:"** marks my own arithmetic. Everything else is quoted or transcribed from the cited page.

## 0. Correction: the six "budget books" are Oakland Schools' budgets, not TSD's

The six "Budget Book / Complete Budget" PDFs for FY22 to FY27 are the **Oakland Schools (Oakland ISD)** proposed budgets. They are filed in TSD's board packets because TSD's board passes a resolution on the ISD budget each year (for example, "TSD Budget resolution - OISD budget support 052124" on 2024-05-21 and "5.D. ISD BUDGET 051623 resolution-support" on 2023-05-16).
- FY27 book, PDF p2: "Oakland Schools Enterprise-Wide Overview … Oakland Schools is one of 56 Intermediate Schools Districts (ISDs)…" **[S25 p2]**
- FY26 book, p1: "Oakland Schools / Original Budget / for the fiscal year 2025-26" **[S26 p1]**
- The dollar figures in these books belong to Oakland Schools, not TSD. Example: "Total MPSERS Retirement Costs … $21,636,800 [2025-26] … $21,049,200 [2026-27]" and "The blended retirement rate expectation for fiscal year 2026-27 is 26.53%" **[S25 p30]**.

**How I use them:** only for the **statewide MPSERS employer-rate tables** they print. Those rates are the same for every K-12 employer, and they match the rate ranges in TSD's own audits exactly (section 2).

**TSD's own budget documents are different files:**
- the June budget-hearing presentations;
- the December, January, March and June amendment presentations;
- the adoption resolutions;
- the February 2026 "4-Year General Fund Forecast".

None of these states a total MPSERS dollar budget or a 147c revenue budget. They disclose MPSERS items only as rate assumptions and as amendment-level changes.

The statewide plan-by-plan tables in the Oakland Schools books give the full structure. The table uses three columns from them:
- **Basic/MIP with Premium Subsidy**: TSD's "effective rate".
- **DC with PHF**: a defined-contribution member, for whom the employer pays only the UAAL charge, so this rate isolates the **district-paid UAAL**.
- **147c UAAL Stabilization Rate**: the state-funded pass-through.

| Oct 2020–Sep 2021 | 28.21% | 20.96% | 7.25% | 14.51% | [S30 p34] |
| Oct 2021–Sep 2022 | 28.23% | 20.96% | 7.27% | 15.05% | [S29 p35] |
| Oct 2022–Sep 2023 | 28.23% | 20.96% | 7.27% | 16.65% | [S28 p32] |
| Oct 2023–Sep 2024 | 31.34% | 20.96% | 10.38% | 16.89% | [S27 p35]; also [S28 p33] as a forecast |
| Oct 2024–Sep 2025 | 31.36% | 20.96% | 10.40% | 10.58% | [S26 p36/37]; also [S27 p36] as a forecast |
| Oct 2025–Sep 2026 | 29.91% | **15.21%** | 14.70% | 15.02% | [S25 p38]; also [S26 p36/37] as a forecast |
| Oct 2026–Sep 2027 (Governor's proposal) | 27.51% | 15.21% | 12.30% | 13.51% | [S25 p39]: "forecasted rates … contained in the Governor's proposed FY27 budget" |

**Reading the 20.96% cap.** Before October 2024, the 20.96% district UAAL charge covered **both pension UAAL and OPEB UAAL**. For Oct 2023 to Sep 2024, the audit's lower bounds are 13.90% pension + 7.06% OPEB = 20.96%.
- **Oct 2024:** the OPEB UAAL went to zero and the full 20.96% went to pension UAAL. The district's total rate barely moved (31.34% → 31.36%). The state's 147c rate fell instead (16.89% → 10.58%).
- **Oct 2025:** the cap dropped to 15.21%.

**Calc: split of the Oct 2025–Sep 2026 Basic/MIP w/PS rate.**
- 29.91% = 15.21% UAAL (ends at payoff) + 10.87% pension normal cost (26.08 − 15.21) + 3.83% OPEB (29.91 − 26.08).
- Other plans' pension normal cost: Pension Plus 6.13% (21.34 − 15.21); Pension Plus 2 6.20% (21.41 − 15.21) **[S25 p38]**.
- DC employer costs continue: "DC Employer Mandatory 4.00%", "DC Employer Match" up to 3.00%, and "Personal Healthcare Fund (PHF)" 2.00% **[S25 p38]**.

**TSD's own documents reflect the 20.96% → 15.21% change.**
- TSD's January 2026 slide gives three deltas **[S7 p5]**:
  - "Pension UAAL expenditures for districts reduced by 5.75%" (20.96 − 15.21 = 5.75);
  - "Healthcare premium subsidy expenditures for districts increased by 2.58%" (1.25 → 3.83);
  - "Pension normal cost expenditures for districts increased by up to 1.72%" (9.15 → 10.87; calc: 30.11 − 20.96 = 9.15 and 26.08 − 15.21 = 10.87).
- Calc: net for a Basic/MIP w/PS member is −5.75 + 2.58 + 1.72 = −1.45 points (31.36% → 29.91%).
- Plante Moran's FY2025 management letter: 147a(4) "was completely removed … This funding was removed given the fiscal year 2026 statutory reduction in the MPSERS UAAL required contribution from 20.96 percent to 15.21 percent of covered payroll." **[S5 p13/11]**

Of the PA 127 of 2024 provisions (from the MPSERS valuation):
- TSD documents corroborate the cap cut and the end of the employee 3% (section 3, FY2026).
- I found nothing in TSD documents about the FY2025 floor reset or the removal of the normal-cost floor.

## 4. How TSD books 147c and the other MPSERS categoricals

- **147c is a pass-through recorded gross.** The state's payment is booked as GF state revenue and as an equal retirement expenditure, then remitted to ORS. The audits phrase it as "an allocation of $13,805,287 in revenue received from the State of Michigan and remitted to the System to fund the MPSERS … (UAAL) stabilization rate" **[S1 p53/36]**.
  - TSD's FY2026 amendment adjusts "147c(1) expenditures to match revenue" **[S7 p11]**.
  - TSD has described it as offset since 2015 **[S32 p8]**.
  - Government-wide statements carry it as a deferred inflow: "The $13,805,287 reported as deferred inflows … will be recognized as state appropriations revenue for the year ending June 30, 2027" **[S1 p55/38]**.
- **147c(2) one-time deposits** are the same kind of pass-through: $9,076,463 in FY2023 **[S4 p52/34]** and $2,113,411 in FY2025 **[S2 p53/34]**. "Ultimately, there is no impact to fund balance of the School District." **[S5 p11/9]**
- **147a(1), 147a(4), 27l(4) and 147a(2)** are true offsets: GF revenue with no matching pass-through expenditure.
  - Documented amounts: 147a(4) $5,055,278 in FY2025; 147a(1) $1,007,246 in the FY2026 original budget, then eliminated; 27l(4) $1,326,153 in FY2026; a 147a(2) "$0.5 million" prior-year adjustment in FY2026.
  - 147e (defined-contribution reimbursement) and 147g (employee health-care reimbursement) are offsets too.
  - FY2025 and FY2026 amounts for every one of these are now in section 6, from MDE's State Aid Financial Status Reports. The archive is at `https://mdoe.state.mi.us/samspublic/Home/StatusArchiveReport`; the final report for each year is `ALL<yy>11.pdf`.
- **147g** reimbursed employees for their 3% OPEB contribution in FY2025 and did not fund operations **[S5 p12/10]**. FY2025 amendment: "147g 3% Reimburse ($1.6m)" **[S15 p9]**. FY2026: "$0.6 million reduction in Sec. 147g" **[S1 p65/48]**.

## 5. What MPSERS paying off its UAAL (the 2030 projection) would mean for TSD

### 5.1 Splitting TSD's current cost into continuing and ending parts

Method: I use the rates in TSD's audits and the statewide rate table (section 2), applied to TSD's audited covered payroll. TSD's own documents give no dollar split.

**FY2026 (calc).** TSD-funded MPSERS = **$27,837,681** (section 1b).
- **UAAL portion paid by TSD.** July to September 2025 was at 20.96% and October 2025 to June 2026 at 15.21%.
  - Time-weighted rate: 0.25 × 20.96% + 0.75 × 15.21% = 16.6475%.
  - 16.6475% × $97,218,696 ≈ **$16,184,482**.
  - This assumes payroll is spread evenly across the months, which is an approximation.
- **Continuing portion** (pension normal cost, OPEB, DC/PHF): $27,837,681 − $16,184,482 ≈ **$11,653,199** (≈12.0% of payroll).
  - If all of FY2026 had carried the 15.21% UAAL rate, the remainder would be $27,837,681 − $14,786,964 = $13,050,717.
  - So the continuing cost at FY2026 payroll is about **$11.7M–$13.1M a year**.
- **147c pass-through:** **$13,805,287** in FY2026. It is separate and net-zero to fund balance.

**Going forward.** The district UAAL charge is 15.21% of all MPSERS-reported payroll, including DC members' payroll. It stays 15.21% under the proposed rates for October 2026 **[S25 p39]**.

### 5.2 Annual amount TSD itself would stop paying (calc, range)

| Basis | Arithmetic | Annual amount |
|---|---|---|
| Low: payroll back at FY2025 level (e.g., after the $12M reduction plan) | 15.21% × $92,923,328 | **$14,133,638** |
| Cross-check against the Governor's $1.7B local share | TSD's NPL proportion 0.85944% × $1,700,000,000 | **$14,610,480** |
| Central: FY2026 actual payroll | 15.21% × $97,218,696 | **$14,786,964** |
| High (illustrative): payroll grows 2%/yr to FY2031 | 15.21% × ($97,218,696 × 1.02^5 = $107,337,296) | **$16,326,003** |

**Range: about $14.1 million to $16.3 million a year, with about $14.6–14.8 million at today's payroll.** Each $1 million of payroll moves the figure by $152,100.

The proportion cross-check agrees with the rate-based figure to within about 1%. That supports reading the release's "$1.7B local" as the districts' 15.21%-of-payroll UAAL charge. TSD's proportion is defined as "a projection of its long-term share of contributions to the pension plan relative to the projected contributions of all participating reporting units" **[S1 p54/37]**.

The 147c pass-through (FY2026: $13,805,287) would also end. GF revenue and expenditures would both shrink by roughly that amount. Calc: 6.83% of FY2026 GF expenditures. The net effect on fund balance is zero.

### 5.3 Scale against TSD's budget (calc, using $14.8M)

- **7.31%** of FY2026 actual GF expenditures ($202,195,371).
- **7.65%** of FY2027 adopted GF expenditures ($193,342,088).
- About **6.5×** the FY2027 budgeted deficit of $(2,286,579).
- About **2.4–2.5×** the February 2026 forecast's operating deficits: $(5,838,185) for 26-27, $(6,190,566) for 27-28, $(5,848,198) for 28-29.
- About **1.23×** the "$12 million in reductions" target.
- **Fund balance:** the FY2027 projected ending balance is $25,938,738 (13.42%), against the board's "15% fund balance" target.
  - Calc: the gap is 0.15 × $193,342,088 − $25,938,738 = **$3,062,575**.
  - One year of UAAL relief would be about 4.8× that gap.

**Timing problem:** TSD's forecast ends in FY2029. The relief would arrive after it (section 5.4), so it does not help the FY2027–FY2029 deficits TSD is cutting for now.

### 5.4 Timing: MPSERS fiscal year vs. TSD fiscal year

- **If "full funding by 2030" means UAAL payments end with MPSERS FY2030 (Sep 30, 2030):**
  - The first UAAL-free rate year starts October 1, 2030.
  - TSD FY2031 (July 2030–June 2031) would get about 9 months: calc 0.75 × $14.8M ≈ $11.1M.
  - TSD FY2032 would be the first full year.
- **If 2030 instead refers to a valuation date:** valuations set rates with a lag. The MPSERS valuations show the 9/30/2025 valuation sets FY2028 rates. On that reading, the relief could start around October 2032 (TSD FY2033).
- **Neither reading is confirmed** in any TSD document.

### 5.5 Caveats

1. **The state may not let districts keep it.** TSD's documents show the state reshaping MPSERS relief from year to year:
   - FY2025's $598M arrived as a categorical (147a(4)) that TSD called "Essentially one-time money" **[S7 p4]**. It was paid "[r]ather than a traditional increase to the foundation allowance" **[S3 p28/9]**, and "essentially replaces an increase in the foundation allowance" **[S6 p12/10]**.
   - FY2026 delivered the cap cut but removed 147a(4) and, "in an unanticipated change", 147a(1) **[S5 p13/11]**.
   - The state could cut 147c, other MPSERS categoricals, or foundation-allowance growth to capture the savings.
   - The School Aid Fund is also under pressure: "this budget continues to shift significant funding from the School Aid Fund (approximately $1.3 billion in 2026) to colleges and universities" **[S5 p12/10]**. TSD's FY2026 MD&A flags the same SAF-to-higher-education trend **[S1 p27/9]**.
2. **The continuing costs persist:** pension normal cost (Basic/MIP 10.87%, Pension Plus 6.13%, Pension Plus 2 6.20%), the OPEB premium-subsidy rate (3.83%, or 1.43% under the Oct 2026 proposal), DC employer contributions (4% mandatory plus up to 3% match), and PHF 2% **[S25 p38–39]**. Calc: about $11.7–13.1M a year at FY2026 payroll.
3. **The UAAL can come back.** TSD's NPL swings from $89.3M to $271.7M across a ±1-point discount-rate range **[S1 p57/40]**. Investment returns drove the recent improvement: "15.5 percent for the year ended September 30, 2024, compared to 8.3 percent" **[S5 p16/14]**.
4. **Payroll base.** I assume "covered payroll" in the RSI is the payroll the UAAL rate applies to. Calc check: FY2026 147c $13,805,287 ÷ $97,218,696 = 14.20%. The time-weighted 147c rate is 0.25 × 10.58% + 0.75 × 15.02% = 13.91%, which is close.
5. **Timing:** the MPSERS year (Oct–Sep) and TSD year (Jul–Jun) differ, and there is a valuation lag (section 5.4).
6. **What the $1.7B split implies.** The release's split matches TSD's share of the district-paid UAAL (section 5.2). That only means districts currently bear about $1.7B. It does not guarantee TSD keeps its about $14.6–14.8M; that depends on future School Aid budgets (caveat 1).

## 6. What TSD's documents say about the state's claims

1. **"$598M a year passed to schools starting FY2025" is structurally supported, with qualifications.**
   - Plante Moran confirms the $598M went out as 147a(4) in FY2025 ($5,055,278 to TSD) **[S5 p11/9]**. It also confirms that FY2026 replaced it with the statutory cap cut from 20.96% to 15.21% **[S5 p13/11]**.
   - However, TSD's own administration characterizes the outcome differently:
     - "Retirement and health-care costs once covered by State have been shifted to the employer" **[S9 slide 2]**;
     - "Employees no longer contributing 3% healthcare premium, which has been absorbed by the employer" **[S7 p5]**;
     - an FY2026 "RETIREMENT ADJUSTMENT 3,612,635 … unanticipated increases in employer contribution rates" **[S7 p11]**.
   - **Calc: TSD-funded MPSERS net of ALL state MPSERS offsets** (corrected 2026-11-18; amounts from MDE's State Aid Financial Status Reports for Troy, 63150).
     - FY2025 offsets: 147a(1) $1,007,246.18 + 147a(2) $3,503,425.81 + 147a(4) $5,055,278.16 + 147e $404,529.41 + 147g $1,634,235.39 = $11,604,714.95 **[MDE 2024-25 status report, p. 1127/1779]**.
     - FY2026 offsets: 147a(2) $3,673,667.34 + 147e $397,455.73 **[MDE 2025-26 status report, p. 1128/1780]** + 27l(4) $1,326,153 **[S7 p8]** = $5,397,276.07. The 2025-26 report has no 147a(1), 147a(4) or 147g line.
     - FY2025: $30,371,009 − $11,604,715 = $18,766,294, or **20.20%** of payroll.
     - FY2026: $27,837,681 − $5,397,276 = $22,440,405, or **23.08%** of payroll.
     - **Troy's own MPSERS cost rose by $3,674,111 (+2.89 points of payroll)** in the first, partial (9-month) year of the lower cap. Gross TSD-funded cost fell $2,533,328, but the state's offsets fell $6,207,439.
     - This matches TSD's own description ("Retirement and health-care costs once covered by State have been shifted to the employer").
     - The status report's 147c(1) amounts match the audits to the dollar ($9,297,728.91 and $13,805,286.56), which supports pairing the state FY report with the same-numbered TSD FY.
     - **Superseded:** the first version of this calc subtracted only 147a(4) (FY2025) and 27l(4) (FY2026) and reported 27.24% vs. 27.27%, "essentially unchanged". It omitted 147a(1) and 147g in FY2025, and 147a(2) and 147e in both years.
   - **Calc: gross relief from the cap cut.** 5.75% × $97,218,696 × 9/12 ≈ $4.19M in TSD FY2026; a full year ≈ $5.59M. The FY2025 147a(4) was $5,055,278. 147a(4) "represented approximately 5.75 percent of the School District's 2024 MPSERS-related payroll" **[S5 p11/9]**.
2. **The funding progress is real in TSD's audits.**
   - Pension fiduciary net position rose from 60.77% (9/30/2022) to 79.44% (9/30/2025).
   - TSD's NPL share fell from $340,447,659 to $172,171,957 **[S1 p61/44]**.
3. **The OPEB funded ratio differs by measure.** TSD's audit (GASB 75 basis) shows OPEB fiduciary net position at **164.94%** of total OPEB liability at 9/30/2025 **[S1 p63/46]**. The 150.64% in the OPEB actuarial valuation is the funding basis. They are different measures, but both are well above 100%.
4. **TSD has called MPSERS categorical changes neutral before.** In 2016 and 2017, TSD's budgets said "MPSERS Categorical changes are expected to have no net impact on the bottom-line budget" **[S34 p231; S35 p31]**. Historically TSD has treated state MPSERS money as offsetting, not as new operating money.
5. **The state's own subsidy grew over time.** The 2015 packet: "The state portion of the MPSERS contribution rate is $675 million in FY 2014-15 and is expected to increase to $908 million in FY 2015-16" **[S33 p138]**.
